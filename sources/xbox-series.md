# Xbox Series X/S

Suggest: RES=1920x1080 FPS=60
Status: reported -
Wake: press the Xbox button on the controller

- Allow HDR10 and Auto HDR: off (Settings › General › TV & display options › Advanced › Video modes). The card captures SDR.
- Allow Variable Refresh Rate: off (Settings › General › TV & display options › Advanced › Video modes). Keeps the signal at a fixed rate.
- Refresh rate: 60 Hz (Settings › General › TV & display options › Refresh rate). Keeps 120 Hz out of the signal.
- Resolution: 1080p (Profile & system › Settings › General › TV & display options › Resolution, path from a third-party guide). The card takes 1080p.
- Bitstream Out: off (Settings › General › Volume & audio output › Speaker Audio › HDMI Audio, path from a third-party guide). Bitstream sends Dolby or DTS, not the PCM stereo the card takes.
