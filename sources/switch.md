# Nintendo Switch

Suggest: RES=1920x1080 FPS=60
Status: reported -
Wake: press the HOME button on the controller

- TV Resolution: 1080p (System Settings › TV Settings › TV Resolution). 1080p is the highest option the Switch offers docked.
- RGB Range: Automatic (System Settings › TV Settings › RGB Range). Change it only if the TV's manual asks for Full or Limited.

- [Nintendo: How to Adjust the TV Settings](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/22331): TV Settings path, TV Resolution options and RGB Range
