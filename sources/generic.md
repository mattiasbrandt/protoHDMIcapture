# Any HDMI source

Suggest:
Status: reported -

- HDCP: Off. With copy protection on, capture cards show black or an error; consoles call it "Enable HDCP" or "HDCP".
- Audio: PCM stereo, not bitstream. Dolby or DTS passthrough gives silence or noise.
- HDR: Off. The card captures SDR.
- Resolution: what the card captures (its best mode in the wizard), usually 1080p. "4K" on the box is the loop-out.
- Refresh: 60 Hz. Turn 120 Hz output and VRR off.
- Black picture? The source is asleep, or HDCP is on.
