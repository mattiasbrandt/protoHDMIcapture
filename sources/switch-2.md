# Nintendo Switch 2

Suggest: RES=1920x1080 FPS=60
Status: reported -

- TV Resolution: 1080p (System Settings › Display › TV Resolution). 4K output is capped at 60 fps, and the card takes 1080p.
- HDR Output: Off (System Settings › Display › HDR Output). The card captures SDR.
- 120 Hz Output: Off (System Settings › Display › 120 Hz Output). 120 Hz is only supported at 1080p and 1440p, and the card takes 60.
- RGB Range: Automatic (System Settings › Display › RGB Range). Change it only if the TV's manual asks for Full or Limited.

- [Nintendo: How to Adjust the Display Settings on Nintendo Switch 2](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/68188): Display path, TV Resolution, HDR Output, 120 Hz Output and RGB Range
