# MacroSilicon MS2109

Ids: 534d:2109
Vendors: 534d
Link: 480
Best: MJPEG 1280x720 @ 60
Hint: MJPEG is effectively required: YUYV manages 5 to 10 fps

Status: 534d:2109 tested 2026-10-16 PS5: picture and audio at MJPEG 720p60 in the Rust viewer

The USB 2.0 chip in the small, cheap HDMI capture sticks. Everything goes
through MJPEG, so each frame is decoded on the PC.

## Quirks

- USB 2.0 only: 480 Mbit/s is the chip's own link, not a fault.
- MJPEG gives 720p60 and 1080p30 (measured 60 and 29.5 fps); YUYV manages 5 fps at 1080p and 10 at 720p.
- Streams uniformly black frames at full rate with nothing on its HDMI input, so frames alone do not prove a signal.
- Streams black (Y about 7) for about 7 s after each stream start, with a source playing, before the picture arrives: a probe shorter than that sees only black.
- Reports no USB serial, so it is pinned by its USB port (`CARD_PORT`).
- Some units declare 96 kHz mono audio that is really 48 kHz stereo; the Linux kernel remaps it. Seen here as plain 48 kHz stereo.
- Exposes the chip's factory HID interface, which ms-tools uses for XDATA and EEPROM access.
- Its JPEGs are read as JPEG defines them, BT.601 and full range: ffmpeg tags them so for mpv, and the Rust viewer's `LEVELS=auto` does the same (#17). With a PS5 the picture spans Y 0 to 255 (2026-10-16), so full range is right; `LEVELS=limited` overrides it.
- The Rust viewer decodes each frame on the CPU (zune-jpeg): about 2.3 ms at 720p and 5.1 ms at 1080p per frame, measured offline on a Ryzen 9 7950X3D (`mjpeg::tests::decode_cost`). Frames without Huffman tables get the standard ones.
